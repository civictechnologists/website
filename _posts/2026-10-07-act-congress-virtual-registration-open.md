### Come join us in Orlando - virtually or in-person!

We're less than two weeks out from our first in-person conference! In person in Orlando, which you can [register for here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/act-congress-2026-tickets-1996060520750). We're also having a [free virtual option](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc-crXpcbFwnAdLJO1X1_xgsU82ddVNwnxvSQRmVffXs5rv_A/viewform) that you can [register for here](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc-crXpcbFwnAdLJO1X1_xgsU82ddVNwnxvSQRmVffXs5rv_A/viewform)!

Hosted this year by [Florida Community Innovation (FCI)](https://floridainnovation.org/), this annual gathering brings together civic technologists, community advocates, nonprofit leaders, and volunteers from across the country to connect, collaborate, and build technology for social good, with unconference sessions, hands-on volunteering opportunities, and skill-building workshops led by ACT leaders. This will be a unique moment to learn, share ideas, and strengthen the civic tech community.

[You can register for the in-person event here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/act-congress-2026-tickets-1996060520750)!

Hope to see you there!

-- The ACT Team
