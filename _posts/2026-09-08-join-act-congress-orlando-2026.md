The Alliance of Civic Technologists is proud to announce our first in-person conference happening on October 17th and 18th in Orlando, Florida at [The Conduit](https://www.credoconduit.com/).

Hosted this year by [Florida Community Innovation (FCI)](https://floridainnovation.org/), this annual gathering brings together civic technologists, community advocates, nonprofit leaders, and volunteers from across the country to connect, collaborate, and build technology for social good, with unconference sessions, hands-on volunteering opportunities, and skill-building workshops led by ACT leaders. This will be a unique moment to learn, share ideas, and strengthen the civic tech community.

[You can register for the event here](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/act-congress-2026-tickets-1996060520750)!

We have a room block with the DoubleTree Downtown Orlando (60 S Ivanhoe Blvd, Orlando, FL 32804). [You can book with the hotel through our link here](https://www.hilton.com/en/book/reservation/deeplink/?ctyhocn=MCOODDT&groupCode=CDT90Z&arrivaldate=2026-10-16&departuredate=2026-10-18&cid=OM,WW,HILTONLINK,EN,DirectLink).

The night before the conference, our sponsor, FCI, will provide light bites at The Greek Corner (1600 N Orange Ave, Orlando, FL 32804) starting at 7 PM ET.

Day 1 of the conference will follow a formal agenda, and yes — if you'd like to propose a session, please reach out to the team at ACT ([congress@civictechnologists.org](mailto:congress@civictechnologists.org)) to share your idea by September 14th! We will also have local nonprofits on site with tech problems they need help with, thanks to a partnership with the Central Florida Foundation and Rally, Orange County, and the City of Orlando's Social Enterprise Accelerator.
